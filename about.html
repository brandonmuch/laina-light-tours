About Laina Light Tours

We know where the light falls.

Laina Light Tours is a Victoria Falls-based tour operator specialising in guided activities, adventures and packages across one of the world’s most extraordinary destinations.

We work with trusted local partners to connect visitors with Victoria Falls on their own terms, whether that is a sunrise flight over the gorge, an afternoon on the Zambezi, or a full-day safari. Every booking is handled personally, with flexible options for individuals, couples and groups.
