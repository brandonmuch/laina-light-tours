Terms of Service

Last updated 1 September 2026

1. Who we are

Laina Light Tours is a tour operator based in Victoria Falls, Zimbabwe, arranging activities, transfers and multi-day packages across the Victoria Falls and Zambezi region, including cross-border experiences in Zambia and Botswana.

These terms apply to every booking made through this website, by WhatsApp, by email or by telephone.

2. Bookings and confirmation

An enquiry sent through this website or by WhatsApp is a request, not a confirmed booking. A booking is confirmed only once we have acknowledged it in writing and the required deposit has been received.

All experiences are subject to availability, minimum numbers, weather and operator schedules. Where a third-party operator runs an activity, that operator’s own safety rules and conditions also apply.

3. Prices, park fees and surcharges

Prices on this site are quoted in United States dollars (USD) per person unless stated otherwise, and are the prevailing rates at the time of publication.

  • Where an activity states that park fees are included, no further park fee is payable.
  • Certain scenic flights carry a separate government and fuel surcharge, shown on the activity itself.
  • Cross-border experiences may require visas and border fees, which are payable by you.
  • Rates may change without notice where an operator or authority revises its own charges.

4. Payment

A deposit is required to confirm a booking, with the balance due before the experience begins unless we have agreed otherwise in writing. We will confirm accepted payment methods when your booking is issued.

5. Cancellations and changes

Cancellation terms vary by experience because they follow the terms of the operator delivering it. The applicable terms are confirmed in writing at the time of booking.

We may cancel or reschedule an experience for reasons of safety, weather, water levels, mechanical fault or insufficient numbers. Where this happens we will offer a reasonable alternative, a reschedule, or a refund of amounts paid for the affected experience.

Seasonal activities — including white-water rafting and some river experiences — operate only in certain months, subject to river conditions.

6. Adventure activities, health and safety

Several experiences we arrange are adventure activities carrying inherent risk, including bungee jumping, gorge and bridge swings, ziplines, white-water rafting, microlight and helicopter flights, and wildlife encounters.

You must disclose any medical condition, pregnancy, injury or mobility limitation that may affect your participation, and you must meet the age, weight and health requirements of the operator. Operators may refuse participation on safety grounds, and may do so without refund where a requirement was not disclosed.

Wildlife experiences take place in open, natural environments. Animal sightings can never be guaranteed, and guide instructions must be followed at all times.

7. Insurance

Comprehensive travel insurance is strongly recommended for every traveller, and should cover medical treatment, emergency evacuation, cancellation and adventure activities. We do not provide insurance.

8. Passports, visas and borders

You are responsible for holding valid travel documents, visas and any required vaccination certificates. Experiences in Zambia or Botswana involve an international border crossing; entry remains at the discretion of the relevant immigration authority.

9. Transfers

Transfer prices are quoted per vehicle unless stated otherwise. Please provide accurate flight numbers and arrival times. We monitor scheduled flights and will wait a reasonable period for delays, but cannot be held responsible for missed connections arising from incorrect information supplied to us.

10. Liability

Laina Light Tours curates and coordinates experiences that are operated by carefully selected, independently licensed partners — flight operators, river and safari operators, guides and transport providers. We select those partners, hold them to our standards, arrange your booking and remain your point of contact throughout.

Each partner remains responsible for the safe operation of its own activity, including its equipment, crew, licensing and insurance. Accordingly, we are not liable for loss, injury, delay or damage arising from the act or omission of a partner operator, or from events outside our reasonable control — including weather, river conditions, wildlife behaviour, mechanical failure, border decisions or civil disruption.

Nothing in these terms excludes any liability that cannot lawfully be excluded.

11. Assumption of risk and indemnity

By booking an adventure activity you acknowledge that it carries inherent risk that cannot be designed away, and you accept that risk on your own behalf and on behalf of any minor in your care.

You agree to indemnify and hold harmless Laina Light Tours, its directors, employees and representatives against any claim, loss, cost or expense arising from:

  • your participation in an activity, other than to the extent caused by our own negligence;
  • a failure to disclose a medical condition, pregnancy, injury or limitation relevant to your participation;
  • a failure to follow the safety instructions of a guide or operator;
  • damage you cause to a vehicle, vessel, equipment or property; or
  • your act or omission causing loss to another guest or a third party.

You may be asked to sign an operator’s own indemnity or waiver before an activity begins. That document is between you and that operator, and signing it is generally a condition of participation.

This clause does not limit any right you have that cannot lawfully be limited.

12. Complaints

If something is not right, please tell your guide or contact us immediately so we can address it while you are still with us. Complaints raised after departure are considerably harder to resolve.
