# Transfers page — images still needed

Drop files straight into this folder. Lowercase, hyphens, no spaces.
Target 1600x1067 (3:2), .webp, under 300 KB.

## Priority order

1. `fleet-exterior-01.webp` — main vehicle, side-on or three-quarter,
   daylight, clean. Bush or the Falls behind it rather than a wall or
   a workshop. This becomes the transfers hero.

2. `driver-name-board.webp` — the driver standing beside the vehicle
   holding a name board. This single image does more for arrival
   anxiety than any amount of copy. Highest-converting transfer shot
   there is.

3. `interior-seats.webp` — cabin from the open door: legroom, clean
   upholstery, air-conditioning vents visible. Families and anyone
   booking a long transfer look for this.

4. `luggage-space.webp` — boot open with real bags in it, not empty.
   Answers "will our cases fit" without a word.

5. `fleet-large-group.webp` — minibus or larger vehicle if the fleet
   has one. Needed for the group-quote panel; without it that section
   runs on type alone.

## Also useful
