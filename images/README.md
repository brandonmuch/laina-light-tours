# Image drop folders

Drop new photography into the matching folder. Filenames become
part of the URL, so use lowercase and hyphens: `land-cruiser-01.jpg`,
never `Land Cruiser (1).JPG`.

## Specs

| Use | Size | Format | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hero / full-bleed | 2400 × 1350 | `.webp` (fallback `.jpg`) | Keep the subject off-centre; the left third carries copy |
| Card / activity | 1200 × 900 | `.webp` | 4:3, subject centred |
| Vehicle | 1600 × 1067 | `.webp` | 3:2, shot side-on or three-quarter |
| Portrait / team | 1000 × 1250 | `.webp` | 4:5 |
| Testimonial avatar | 200 × 200 | `.webp` | Square, face centred |

Target under 300 KB each. Anything over 500 KB will slow the site
noticeably on mobile data, which is how most international visitors
will first see it.

## Folders

### `vehicles/`  ← transfers page
Their own fleet. Wanted, roughly in priority order:
1. Vehicle side-on in daylight, clean, ideally with the Falls or
   bush behind it rather than a wall or workshop.
2. Interior — seats, legroom, air-conditioning. Reassures families
   and long-transfer bookers more than any exterior shot.
3. Luggage space with bags actually in it.
4. Driver beside the vehicle holding a name board. This is the
   single most trust-building transfer image there is.
5. Larger vehicle / minibus if the fleet has one, for group quotes.

### `hero/`
Full-bleed page headers. Per the visual direction: golden hour,
atmospheric haze, mist, people small against the landscape.

### `culture/`
Artisans, baskets, textiles, markets, musicians, guides, food.
Grounded in Victoria Falls and Zimbabwe specifically.

### `team/`
Guides and consultants. Candid and working, not posed head-shots
against a backdrop.

### `testimonials/`
Guest faces to sit beside written reviews. Only with permission.
